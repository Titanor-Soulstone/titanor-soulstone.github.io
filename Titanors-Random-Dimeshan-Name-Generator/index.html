Titanor's Random Dimeshan Name Generator

Given names

Dimeshan names are unisex, and they are for both male and female characters.

They are generated by combining a pre-sylable and a post-sylable, with an optional secondary pre-sylable in between. The pre-sylables are two letter combinations that make up the first one or two sections of a given name, while the post-sylables are three to four letter combinations that make up the last section of a given name.

Last names

Dimeshan last names are generated by combining two last name parts, which are common words or syllables that are used in Dimeshan last names. The first part is capitalized, while the second part is lowercase.
